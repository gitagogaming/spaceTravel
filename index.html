GAME INSTRUCTIONS:

  • Objective: Dodge the asteroids with your spaceship.
  • Game Over: If an asteroid hits your spaceship, the game is over.
  • Collect Points: Click on the antimatter to collect points.
  • Controls: Use the left and right buttons to move the spaceship.
  • Shooting: Click anywhere or press Spacebar to shoot bullets straight up.
  • PowerUps: Collect blue angle shooting power-ups to aim in any direction, and orange multi-shot power-ups to fire three bullets at once!
  • Score: Earn 5 points for each asteroid you destroy!
